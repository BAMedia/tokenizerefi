How it works

Three steps, with the county record always in charge.

We add a verified digital layer on top of your home's public record. We don't replace it, and we never touch ownership.

1

Verify your home

Confirm your property against the county's recorded data. The county record stays the legal source of truth.

2

Create its property identity

We mint a digital twin of your home's identity, with key facts and a dated assessed value in its metadata. It is a record of identity, not a transfer of ownership rights.

3

Protect it and explore options

Get deed-fraud alerts, and when you're ready, explore ways to access your home equity or refinance through licensed partners.

Why property identity matters

Your home is likely your largest asset. Its identity deserves protection.

Deed and title fraud happens when someone falsely records a transfer or borrows against a home they don't own. A verified property identity makes those changes easier to spot early.

  • Deed-fraud protectionMonitoring for unexpected filings tied to your property, with alerts so you can act quickly.
  • A dated, tamper-evident recordYour property identity carries key facts and an "assessed value as of" date, so everyone sees the same snapshot.
  • Built with counties, not around themWe integrate county by county to assist recorders and clerks. We are not a registrar, and the county record remains the legal record.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this a loan offer?

No. TokenizeRefi is not a lender and this page is not a loan offer or solicitation. Joining the waitlist simply lets us contact you as the service opens in your area. Any financing would be offered only by licensed partners, subject to their own terms and approval.

Does tokenizing my home transfer ownership?

No. Your property identity is a digital twin of your home's identity, not a deed and not ownership rights. Nothing about your title or ownership changes.

Who holds the official record?

Your county. The county recorder's record remains the legal source of truth. We work county by county to assist that record, not to replace it.

What is the "assessed value" in my property identity?

It's a dated snapshot drawn from public assessment data, and sometimes an appraisal where one exists, labeled "as of" its date. It is for reference only and is not a valuation or an offer of any kind.
